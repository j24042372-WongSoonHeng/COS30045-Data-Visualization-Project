Epidemiological Disparities

Pedestrian Risk: First Nations people represent almost double the proportion of pedestrian injuries (12.1% vs 6.8%), frequently linked to rural/remote road conditions with limited pedestrian barriers.

Admission Growth: Annual hospitalisations grew from 1,150 in 2011 to 2,214 in 2021 (+92.5%), highlighting critical road trauma health equity challenges.
